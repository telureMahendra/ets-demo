1. Summary

  • The Game is free to play and does not require you to create an account, register or log in.
  • We do not ask you for, and the Game does not directly collect, personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address or passwords.
  • The Game displays advertisements provided by third parties. Those advertising providers may automatically process certain technical and advertising-related information from your device, as described below.

2. No accounts or credentials

The Game does not require any login, user account or registration. The Game does not directly request or collect any of the following from you:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Passwords or other login credentials

We do not operate a developer-owned database of player profiles or personal information for the Game.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The Game is free and is supported by advertising. Advertisements may be shown, for example, after a game ends, when you choose to continue, return or revive a game, or when you use certain game-related features.

To show ads, the Game includes software development kits (SDKs) provided by third-party advertising providers. These providers operate independently of us. When ads are loaded or displayed, they may automatically process certain technical or advertising-related information in order to provide, measure, personalize, limit or improve advertising, and to help prevent fraud and abuse. Depending on the provider and your device settings, this information may include:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID (which you can reset or opt out of in your device settings)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and operating system details (for example model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • App information (such as the app's name and version) and network or carrier information
  • Ad interaction data (such as ads shown, clicked or closed) and diagnostic or performance data

These providers may use this information under their own privacy policies, and we do not control how they do so. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of the advertising providers used in the Game. If an ad takes you to a website or another app, that destination has its own privacy practices.

Where an advertising provider offers it, Android lets you control ad personalization through your device's settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Ads, where available, to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization).

4. Information we collect vs. information processed by advertising providers

Information collected directly by Empire SoftTech

The Game does not directly collect personal information from you. We do not receive your name, contact details or credentials through the Game. If you choose to contact us (for example by email), we will receive the information you send us, and we will use it only to respond to you.

Information processed automatically by third-party advertising providers

The advertising SDKs in the Game may collect and process the technical and advertising-related information described in Section 3 directly from your device. This processing is carried out by those providers, not by us, and is governed by their own policies and terms. We do not use that information to identify you personally.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by the law of your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Game contains third-party advertising, we ask parents and guardians to supervise the use of the Game by minors and to use their device's parental controls and ad settings as appropriate.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details in Section 10 and we will take reasonable steps to address it.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Game and to avoid collecting personal information we do not need. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We also cannot control the security practices of the third-party advertising providers whose SDKs are included in the Game.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the Game has no user accounts and we do not maintain a database of players' personal information, there is no account or profile that we hold about you that we could retain or delete. Any game data stored on your device (such as scores or settings) stays on your device and can be removed by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling the Game.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is retained according to their own policies. To limit it, you can reset your Android advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings, and you can contact those providers directly about their data. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to respond to you and to meet any legal obligations; you may ask us to delete it at any time.

8. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable privacy laws (for example, in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, the GDPR; in certain U.S. states, state privacy laws). These may include the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to withdraw consent, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

As we do not directly collect personal information through the Game, there is generally no personal information held by us to access, correct or delete. For information processed by advertising providers, you may exercise your choices by:

  • Resetting your advertising ID or opting out of ads personalization in your Android device settings;
  • Contacting the relevant advertising provider about its processing; or
  • Uninstalling the Game.

You may also contact us with any privacy question or request, and we will do our best to help or direct you to the right provider.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the Game or the services it uses change, or to reflect legal requirements. When we do, we will publish the updated version on this page and update the effective date at the top. Please review this page periodically. Your continued use of the Game after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
